## Question for the developer
- **QD-A — Erasure of guests (ARC-2).** Proposal: "Eliminati di recente" keeps guests until you choose "Elimina definitivamente" (one or all); no automatic expiry. Alternative: automatic erasure after 30 days via the existing daily cron (more code, data disappear without action).

## ADR
Yes: ADR-0002 "Dati personali degli invitati" (audit exclusion, hard delete, no anon access, R-04 token pattern) — it sets the precedent for R-04 and R-06.
